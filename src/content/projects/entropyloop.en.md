# Entropy Loop

**Entropy Loop** is a 3D video game combining **Deck-Building** and **Auto-Battler** mechanics (inspired by Teamfight Tactics).

Developed as part of a end-of-studies project within a team of 6 developers, we took on the challenge of handling the Art Direction and 3D asset creation ourselves—fields we weren't initially familiar with—in order to expand our skill set.

As the Lead Developer on this project, I managed the overall software architecture, implemented the automated combat systems, developed the core game mechanics, and optimized 3D performance under Unity.

## How the Game Works

**Core Gameplay Loop (Auto-Battler & Deck-Building):** Much like TFT, players build their army beforehand and during preparation phases using their card deck. Once the combat phase starts, units automatically engage and fight enemy waves.

---

# Entities & Art Direction

Due to the lack of specialized artists on the team, we opted for a **Low-Poly** art direction, allowing us to produce a cohesive set of units and environments while maintaining clear readability during combat.

# Map

Each level's map is entirely **procedurally generated**.

No terrain prefabs are manually placed: the entire topography, obstacles, and map structure rely on **mathematical calculations and formulas** executed at runtime. This ensures infinite replayability and unique environments for every single match.
